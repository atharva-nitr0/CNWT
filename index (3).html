Welcome to the Inspection and Data Analyser System

🔍 Screw Detection Overview

Our Inspection and Data Analyser system utilizes a live video feed to monitor and detect screws in real-time. By analyzing the video frames, the system counts the number of screws passing through, distinguishes between good quality screws and defective ones, and maintains a live record of the inspection process. This assists in ensuring high-quality standards and minimizing defective products reaching the final assembly line.

⚙️ Process Description

  1. Video Capture: The system captures live video using a camera positioned over the inspection area.
  2. Screw Detection: Objects resembling screws are detected using advanced processing techniques.
  3. Classification: Each detected screw is classified as either 'Good' or 'Defective' based on predefined parameters.
  4. Data Update: Counts for Total, Good, and Defective screws are updated in real-time on the dashboard.
  5. Graphical Representation: Analysis of the inspection data is visualized using a bar graph in the Data Analyser section.

🎯 Objectives

  • Ensure efficient and real-time monitoring of screws during production.
  • Automatically detect and classify screws without manual intervention.
  • Reduce the chances of defective products reaching the next stages.
  • Provide intuitive graphical analytics for quick performance evaluation.
  • Maintain a historical record of inspections for quality audits.
